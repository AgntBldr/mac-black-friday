# Rules

A personal, curated list. Deals that don't fit are closed.

**We hand check every app** to keep the list good. So your app must be downloadable without paying: a free download, free version or free trial. No purchase, license key or invite needed to try it.

- Apps for Mac, iPhone, iPad, Apple Vision Pro or Apple Watch only. Finished and maintained.
- A real Black Friday deal on your own app, with the price and the dates. Starting in November, ending between Nov 20 and Dec 15.
- The regular price has been the real price for at least 20 days before the deal starts.
- Newer price? Your deal can go in Launch Deals: category `launch`, with the launch price as the headline (like "Launch price $9.99").
- One entry per app. Up to five apps per developer.
- Up to five deal files per pull request, nothing else in the diff.

🚫 Not accepted:

- 100% off or "free while in beta"
- "Price goes up soon" deals
- Pre-orders and waitlists
- Shortened links, or links to other deal lists and bundles
- Deals that need a follow, like, subscribe or email to get the code
- Hidden prices, or prices you only see after signing up
- Courses, books, templates, boilerplates, themes and plugins
- Half-finished or AI-slop apps, or Mac apps that aren't signed and notarized
